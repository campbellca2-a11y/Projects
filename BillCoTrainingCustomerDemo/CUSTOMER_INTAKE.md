## 2. Training problem

What must the learner be able to recognize, recall, distinguish, or perform?

What currently causes the most hesitation, confusion, or training time?

What does “good enough to use in class” look like?

## 3. Authoritative source material

Provide only material the agency is authorized to use for this training aid.

Examples:
- approved call-type / Nature / CrimeCode lists
- CAD event type or subtype lists
- geography, car-number, sector, abbreviation, or unit sheets
- approved SOP excerpts
- instructor reference sheets
- approved training scenarios

For each source, identify:
- title / version / effective date
- whether it is current
- who can resolve conflicts or ambiguities

## 4. Training vocabulary

Use the agency's own terms.

What does the agency call:
- the primary event / incident classification?
- subtypes?
- CAD event types?
- sectors / response areas?
- unit or car numbers?
- SOP / policy / procedure references?

List any terms BillCo must not substitute or rename.

## 5. LearningLadder targets

Mark the rungs needed for the first release.

- Orient — references, quick guides, ladders
- Encode — learning hooks, groups, contrasts, anchors
- Retrieve — forward/reverse recall drills
- Discriminate — near-neighbor and exception practice
- Hold — short-term / delayed recall
- Apply — scenario or mixed operational practice

## 6. Validation

Agency identifies one Training POC who can approve:
- source accuracy
- terminology
- answer mappings
- scenario boundaries
- go-live acceptance

BillCo does not treat ambiguous material as permission to guess.

## 7. Delivery

Default pilot delivery:
- one browser URL
- no learner login
- phone and PC friendly
- browser-local learner history when a LearningLedger is included
- static deployment unless another architecture is separately agreed

## 8. Go-live acceptance

Before release, the Training POC confirms:

1. The source set is current enough for the pilot.
2. Required terminology is correct.
3. Material mappings are accurate.
4. The training aid is acceptable for classroom use.
5. Learners are instructed that current agency policy and authorized training control if anything differs from the study aid.
