# Kill Checklist — Scratcher Mechanical Spec v1.0

Purpose:
This checklist exists to rapidly eliminate candidate scratchers or utensils that do not meet the v1.0 mechanical baseline.
Any failure of a HARD STOP is an immediate reject.

---

## 🔴 HARD STOPS (Any NO = Reject Immediately)

### 1. Overall Length
Requirement:
- ≥ **13.0 inches** overall

Result: ☐ Pass ☐ Fail  
Notes:

---

### 2. Bowl Width
Requirement:
- ≥ **3.5 inches** across

Result: ☐ Pass ☐ Fail  
Notes:

---

### 3. Head Base Depth
Requirement:
- **~2.8–3.0 inches** (table → head base)

Result: ☐ Pass ☐ Fail  
Notes:

---

### 4. Total Head Envelope
Requirement:
- **~3.8–4.1 inches** (table → top of head)

Result: ☐ Pass ☐ Fail  
Notes:

---

### 5. Curve Geometry
Requirement:
- **Single continuous progressive arc**
- NO hinge bends
- NO flat segments
- NO abrupt angle changes

Result: ☐ Pass ☐ Fail  
Notes:

---

## 🟡 SOFT FILTERS (Evaluate only if ALL hard stops pass)

### 6. Material Behavior
- Elastic / springy steel
- Not floppy
- Not rigid or brittle

Result: ☐ Acceptable ☐ Concern  
Notes:

---

### 7. Tine Uniformity
- Even length
- Rounded ends
- No sharp points

Result: ☐ Acceptable ☐ Concern  
Notes:

---

### 8. Balance in Hand
- Neutral feel
- No head-heavy or tail-heavy bias

Result: ☐ Acceptable ☐ Concern  
Notes:

---
