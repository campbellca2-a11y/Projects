## Identity
**What this is:** A mechanically empathetic dog scratching tool (utility tool, not novelty)  
**Why it exists:** To deliver consistent, comfortable, effective scratching across coats and body types without aggressive pressure spikes.

---

## Versioning
**This document defines:** Mechanical baseline for v1.0  
**Frozen Components (v1.0):**
- Overall length: **13.5 inches**
- Bowl width: **3.5 inches**
- Curve type: **continuous progressive arc** (no hinge bends / no flats)
- Head geometry targets:
  - Head base depth (table → base): **~2.8–3.0 inches**
  - Total head envelope (table → top): **~3.8–4.1 inches**
  - Effective scratch reach: **~2.1–2.2 inches**
- Functional intent: wrist-neutral leverage, progressive pressure increase, wide contact patch before tine engagement

**Not Frozen (may change freely without version bump):**
- Product name / brand name
- Finish, color, coatings
- Packaging design
- Guarantee copy and marketing text

---

## Unfreeze Rule (Mechanical Spec)
The v1.0 mechanical spec remains frozen **unless**:
1) manufacturing constraints require a revision, OR  
2) repeated dog-response testing shows a consistent problem, OR  
3) durability/safety testing shows a consistent failure mode.

Any mechanical change becomes a new version (v1.1 or v2.0) with written rationale.
