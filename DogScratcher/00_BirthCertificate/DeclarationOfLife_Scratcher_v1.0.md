# Declaration of Life — Scratcher v1.0

On this date, the Scratcher Mechanical Spec v1.0 is recognized as **a real product artifact**, not a loose idea.

This declaration exists to:
- establish a stable baseline (“v1.0”) for comparison, testing, and sourcing
- prevent drift caused by second-guessing or endless iteration
- create an audit trail of intent and decisions

**We freeze the mechanics, not the creativity.**  
Branding, naming, packaging, and copy may evolve without altering the mechanical truth of v1.0.

If v1.0 changes mechanically, it will be versioned, justified, and archived — never overwritten.
