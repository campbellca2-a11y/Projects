# 1️⃣ One-Page MVP Spec (V1.0)

### Product Name (Working)

**Dog Scratcher™**
(Keep it literal for now. Clever comes later.)

### Purpose

A self-service scratching surface that allows dogs to control pressure, location, and duration of scratching without human assistance.

### Target Dog

* Small to large dogs (25–90 lbs)
* Short to medium coat (V1)
* Especially beneficial for seniors, rescues, and mobility-limited dogs

### Core Form

* **Vertical scratch panel**
* **Wall- or corner-mounted**
* Dog rubs neck, shoulders, flank

### Dimensions (V1)

* Height: **18–20 in**
* Width: **6–8 in**
* Depth (including bristles): **1.5–2.5 in**

(These tolerate variation — don’t lock microns.)

### Bristle Surface

* Material: **TPR rubber** or **nylon blend**
* Stiffness: *Medium-firm* (think car interior brush, not toothbrush)
* Rounded tips (no sharp ends)

### Backing Plate

* Material: **ABS or HDPE**
* Slight flex preferred over brittle rigidity

### Mounting (V1)

* **Industrial adhesive strips** (3M VHB class)
* Optional screw holes molded in (future-proofing)

### Safety

* No sharp edges
* No small removable parts
* Bite-resistant but not “chew toy” rated

### Finish

* Neutral color: **dark gray or black**
* No scent
* No moving parts

### What This Is NOT

* Not medical
* Not chew-proof
* Not adjustable (yet)
