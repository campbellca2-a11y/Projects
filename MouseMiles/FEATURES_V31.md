## 🚗 PHASE 2: 3-Mode Odometer

Like a car's dashboard. Switch between three views:

### Master Odometer (Lifetime)
Your all-time total. Never resets (unless you hit the reset button).
- Shows cumulative km/miles
- Lifetime clicks and scroll steps
- "Last reset: Never" (unless you do it)

### Recent Odometer (Today)
Resets daily at midnight. Track your daily grind.
- Today's distance
- Today's clicks and scroll
- "Resets: Daily at 12:00 AM"

### Trip Odometer (Current Session)
This session's movement only.
- Session start time
- Elapsed time in hours/minutes
- Session distance + interactions

**Features:**
- Large, readable mileage display (Courier New, 48pt)
- Metric/Imperial unit toggle
- Updates every 200ms (smooth, car-like)
